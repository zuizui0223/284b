# Niche-demography exploratory program: 9-state completed decision

2026-11-27. Original M1+M2 invited Method manuscript and historical endpoint remain untouched.

- Initial NE regional pilot, three states: 25 taxa, 1,657 reference plots, 1,211 evaluation plots, 9,074 pair-plot records. Stem-count opposition slope +0.516 percentage points per +0.1 D, conditional 95% [-0.373,1.373]. Artifact 15172710996; run 45940389477.
- Fixed geographic replications: central Appalachia 47 taxa, 2,346 reference, 1,384 evaluation plots, 10,728 records; +0.652 pp [0.180,1.122]. Great Lakes 39 taxa, 5,386 reference, 1,973 evaluation plots, 10,556 records; -1.591 pp [-2.440,-0.739]. The regional signs recur for basal area and leave-one-species-out. However both marginal-change-preserving nulls contain the observed slope in their descriptive 95% ranges and actual opposition is lower than null medians. No evidence for excess local compensation. Run 45940587044.
- Total: 9,389 reference plots, 4,568 evaluation plots, 30,358 pair-plot records across nine states. These are not independent species or tests.
- Checksum-locked retrospective common-pair audit reproduced both full regional slopes, verified all 25 original data-object hashes, and restricted both regions to exactly the 27 shared pairs of 12 species with >=20 shared validation plots in each. The original D rank correlation between these pairs in the two regional reference niches is 0.10989. Pooled reference: 7,732 plots, 20 supported climate bins.
- Same-pair regional D slopes: Appalachia -0.900 pp [-2.199,1.081], Great Lakes -1.974 pp [-4.124,0.194]. Same-pair common D with region-specific cooccurrence J: Appalachia -2.612 pp [-4.694,-0.977], Great Lakes +0.231 pp [-2.756,3.287]. Common D and common J: -1.923 pp [-3.732,-0.101] and -0.380 pp [-3.407,2.917]. Basal-area common D with regional J: +1.115 pp [-2.254,4.418] and +4.298 pp [1.848,7.325]. Matched audit has less support: 502 and 421 plots contributing within-plot estimates. Run 45940879055, artifact 15173061456. Original regional positive/negative contrast **is not maintained** under this matched-pair, common-reference test. Do not claim a genuine regional ecological sign switch or universal niche-overlap effect.
- Interpretation: the population-count relationship depends on the species pool and reference niche estimation, and count and basal-area endpoints may diverge. These are exploratory associations and sensitivity diagnostics; no new confirmatory ecological endpoint. No actual temporal climate exposure has been assessed. No causal competition, demographic compensation, or future community trajectory established.

Next ecological question (not yet tested): rather than further threshold searches, test whether baseline climate-niche similarity masks *life-stage-specific* demographic divergence (juvenile recruitment versus adult survival and growth), and whether this predicts subsequent adult composition change in a later nonoverlapping interval. The mechanism requires actual linked tree remeasurements and a separate climate-exposure dataset. Maintain existing frozen M1/M2 manuscript boundaries. Do not promote this pilot to an Ecology Letters biological finding.
