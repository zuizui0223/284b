# Four-census geographic and temporal extension of regeneration forecasts

2026-11-27. Fixed before fetching or viewing new-state outcomes. Previous nine-state study and invited 284b manuscript stay unchanged. This is a geographic extension within FIA, not independent data collection or a formally registered confirmatory trial.

## Question and disconfirming alternatives

Does sapling stock improve adult-sized tree composition forecasts primarily when change is already under way, or does information persist into a later, nonoverlapping interval in previously stable stands? Does smaller versus larger sapling information shift with horizon, consistent with size-transition timing? A trivial contemporaneous prediction gain is insufficient. Changes in forecast difficulty and stock sampling/size structure are alternatives; no claim of competition, climate causation, reproductive maturity or complete demographic mediation.

## Frozen population and outcomes

Two new regions, six states never used in previous nine-state branch: central hardwoods (OH, IN, IL), southern uplands (KY, TN, NC). Download official FIA PLOT, COND, SUBPLOT, TREE ZIPs (same DataMart URLs); record all raw SHA-256 and bytes before opening data. No other regions or threshold replacement. Same national four-subplot/microplot full-area, single forest condition, no recorded nonzero treatment and live-stem validity gates as the three-census analysis. Plot arm 1 only (same deterministic physical-key hash); t0 in 2004–2012, followed by three exact PREV_PLT_CN-linked visits, 4–8 years each, t3 through 2024. Earliest qualifying quadruple per physical plot. Restrict to >=2 species each with >=2 adult-sized stems at t1; require t0 adult total >0 to make prior stability defined. All four censuses qualified; zeros only for complete censuses, not missing links. This balanced design selects persistently observed forests. Recorded treatment exclusions do not prove disturbance absence.

All three endpoints on identical plots and t1-eligible species:
1. Short: t1→t2 change in log adult count with 0.5 offset, scaled to five years.
2. Delayed: t2→t3 change, scaled to five years, **forecast using t0/t1 information only**, no t2 predictor or selection on t2 abundance.
3. Cumulative: t1→t3 change, scaled to five years (actual 8–16 years).
Each outcome centered within the fixed t1 species set. Actual interval lengths reported. This is relative change among initially present species, not complete future colonization or absolute population size.

Same frozen model A (current adult log count, prior adult change, current mean diameter, small-adult fraction, adult abundance/history × stand density, species effects), equal total weight per plot, within-plot centering, five deterministic 1° spatial block folds and ridge penalty 0.01×training weight, training-only RMS scaling. S adds current log1p early (1–<3 in DBH) and late (3–<5 in) sapling counts. H adds prior histories (secondary); E and L each add only corresponding stock, not history. Adult >=5 in, fixed expansion factors and complete area, not reproductive stages. Regional models re-estimated with block-heldout evaluation; not a true later-calendar-year external forecast. No hyperparameter tuning.

Prior stable: adult count-composition TV(t0,t1) ≤0.10; changing otherwise. Evaluate A→S gain in plot-equal MSE across all, stable, changing, for each horizon. Use jointly resampled spatial blocks (1,000 draws, fixed seed) for changing−stable gain, both relative percent and absolute MSE; compare delayed versus short gain and early-versus-late preference (100*(MSE_L−MSE_E)/MSE_A) at identical plots and bootstrap draws. Positive preference favors early stock. Primary geographic recurrence requires short A→S gain and changing-minus-stable absolute gain positive with conditional 95% intervals above zero in both new regions; strong context dependence additionally requires same sign after matching below. A credible long-lead signal requires positive delayed stable A→S gains with intervals above zero in both regions. Report all outcomes, including no support and contradictory signs. No post hoc threshold or state changes.

## Baseline difficulty audit (fixed prior to results)

Match changing and stable plots on preforecast comparability, not on their observed future errors. Within each outer held-out fold estimate predicted short-horizon adult-only error using inner spatial-fold held-out A errors in outer training plots only. Model log(error+1e-4) with ridge on log adult count, richness, log mean DBH, prior turnover and the A forecast amplitude; all held-out features available at t1. No target plot future value used. Use common region-wise tercile bins of predicted log difficulty, terciles of adult count and median of mean DBH; retain strata with >=10 plots of each stability class. Weight each class to the same stratum distribution with weights min(n_stable,n_changing)/n_class. Same matched plots/weights at all horizons. Report discarded support, effective sample size, weighted covariate balance, and conditional spatial block bootstrap for absolute and relative gain contrasts. This controls measured comparability only; not causality or guaranteed complete confounding adjustment. If no support, unresolved, no relaxed bins.

## Secondary interpretation

Report all model comparisons on same rows; early-vs-late horizon contrast distinguishes a possible stage-dependent lag, not causal evidence. Report stock coefficient ranges across folds, actual support, species and block counts, raw source checksums, block-level held-out losses and reproducible code. Mechanistic adult losses and sapling lineage need future dedicated audit (previous 9-state direct link audit cannot be assumed to generalize); this extension tests predictive timing, not full mortality/recruitment decomposition. Climate normals and actual climate anomalies not used here. No empirical increment to prior sealed M1/M2 ledger. The earlier stable/changing contrast is exploratory, now tested in new geographic data.
