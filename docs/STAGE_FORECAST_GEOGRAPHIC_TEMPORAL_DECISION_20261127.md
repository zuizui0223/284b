## Scientific decision

The six-state extension contains 1,343 nonoverlapping evaluated plots across the two cohorts and 6,130 species-plot rows, all within the same FIA network. It does not reproduce the earlier three-region gain or the stability-dependent advantage with the frozen models. Short-horizon failure in a disjoint complementary cohort cannot be dismissed solely as the small four-census panel, though sample support, census cadence, geographic composition, and measurement/eligibility differences remain alternatives. Do not call the earlier finding a universal early warning, a demonstrated biological context mechanism, or a forecast-horizon law. This route is closed as an exploratory nonreplication, not repaired by alternative state/threshold/model selection. New confirmatory empirical ledger increment 0. The original 284b M1/M2 manuscript and its sealed endpoint remain untouched.
