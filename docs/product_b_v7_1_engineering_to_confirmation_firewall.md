# Product-B v7.1 engineering-to-confirmation firewall

This rule was frozen before the first JOS001 engineering one-shot occurrence run reached a terminal outcome.

## JOS001 can never become confirmatory

JOS001 was selected after reviewing the v5-v7 feasibility failures and is permanently engineering-only. No sampling, model, invariant, or knockout result from JOS001 may be counted as confirmatory evidence.

## If the engineering sampling preflight passes

A v7.1 engineering sampling pass requires both:

- the focal host to pass the unchanged 50 independent records / 30 unique 10-km cells / 10 effective 10-km cells floor; and
- at least 5 frozen replacement hosts to pass the same host sampling floor.

If this happens, it only demonstrates that the prospective literature-based host-feasibility screen can generate an empirically executable design. A new confirmatory pair or panel must then be declared from literature without using occurrence counts, maps, cell statistics, JOS001 support values, JOS001 invariant values, or JOS001 process results for candidate selection.

## If the engineering sampling preflight does not pass

If JOS001 terminates as host-sampling unresolved, control-sampling unresolved, transport/infrastructure unresolved, or another pre-invariant unresolved state:

- do not lower the 50/30/10 host floor;
- do not replace controls after seeing their sampling;
- do not change USA framing to a witness-derived or occurrence-derived geometry;
- do not rerun JOS001 to seek a different result;
- do not treat the failure as evidence against the biological dependency or against the invariant;
- record v7.1 as an engineering feasibility endpoint and reconsider the evidence source/measurement architecture rather than rescuing JOS001.

## Downstream boundary

Even if JOS001 sampling is feasible, model fitting and invariant evaluation require a separately frozen and authorized engineering stage. No current JOS001 sampling authorization opens model-fit, invariant, negative-control-outcome, or process-knockout results.
