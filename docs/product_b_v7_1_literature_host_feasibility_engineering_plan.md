# Product-B v7.1 — prospective literature host-feasibility engineering line

## Status and claim boundary

v7.1 is an **engineering successor**, not a confirmatory ecological result. It is introduced only after the v5-v7 feasibility audit showed that arbitrary biologically compelling obligate pairs often fail before the invariant layer, including focal-host failure in 4 of 6 sampling endpoints. No opened v5-v7 endpoint is rescued or reclassified.

A v7.1 engineering pair may show that the execution architecture can cross the sampling and model-pool gates. Such a pass is not evidence for the directional invariant. A later pair chosen after this engineering rule and implementation are frozen is required for a confirmatory invariant test.

## Unchanged scientific contract

- direction remains `Y_requires_X`;
- X occurrence recovery floor remains at least 50 independent records, 30 unique 10-km cells and 10 effective cells;
- Y remains primary-literature witness evidence, at least 5 independent witnesses in at least 3 unique 10-km cells;
- witness coordinates are test-only and cannot define the frame;
- coordinate uncertainty ceiling remains 10 km;
- at least 5 taxonomy- and sampling-adequate replacement hosts are required;
- breadth ceiling remains 0.80 of the later frozen audit frame;
- 100 deterministic shuffled-host draws and q05/q95 classification remain unchanged;
- stage-1 and host-specific stage-2 differentiability gates remain mandatory;
- model/invariant/process-knockout stages remain separately frozen and authorized.

## New prospective feasibility screen

Before any occurrence count/query is opened for a NEW engineering pair, X must additionally satisfy all of the following from independent literature or stable taxonomic/range sources:

1. at least 10 directly documented X/Y association or X population localities with printed latitude/longitude are available in primary literature;
2. those localities span at least 2 named first-order administrative units or equivalent independently named regional units;
3. the evidence is not a project-generated geocode or a GBIF/iNaturalist occurrence export;
4. a named independent administrative, protected-area or ecoregion frame can be declared without using the witness coordinates to derive its geometry;
5. at least 8 biologically plausible replacement-host taxa can be declared before occurrence availability is inspected, so taxonomy loss does not immediately force adaptive pool expansion.

This screen is a feasibility prior, not an occurrence proxy. **GBIF counts, maps, density surfaces, unique-cell counts and model outcomes remain forbidden for candidate selection.**

## Engineering-first / confirmation-later firewall

The first v7.1 pair is explicitly an engineering pilot because the feasibility screen itself was motivated by observed v5-v7 failures. It may be used to debug transport, sampling and stage-2 differentiability implementation, but its eventual invariant result (if reachable) is not the primary confirmatory endpoint.

After the engineering pilot is terminalized, the v7.1 code and admission rule must be frozen again. A different, previously unopened pair must then be selected using the already-frozen rule before any occurrence access for a confirmatory test.

## First engineering pair

`JOS001`: `Tegeticula synthetica` requires `Yucca brevifolia` (western Joshua tree association).

Primary evidence: Yoder et al. 2013, DOI `10.1111/jeb.12134`. The paper describes the Joshua tree-yucca moth interaction as the classic obligate mutualism, states that yucca seeds are the moth larvae's food source, and samples 14 western `T. synthetica` sites with printed coordinates in Table 1. These sites occur in California and Nevada. The three Tikaboo contact-zone sites are excluded from the primary witness registry because host specificity breaks down there.

No occurrence availability was inspected to choose JOS001.
