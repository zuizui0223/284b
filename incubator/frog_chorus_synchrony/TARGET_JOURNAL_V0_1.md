## Primary target — Journal of Animal Ecology, Research Article

### Why it fits

The current Journal of Animal Ecology scope explicitly covers community, individual and
behavioural animal ecology and prioritizes studies that test broad ecological principles
and generate conclusions extending beyond one species or region.

The frog programme now has the features needed to make that case:

- a general community-level question: environmental pulses and temporal niche overlap;
- two independent continents and monitoring designs;
- prospectively frozen primary and validation rules;
- negative mechanism tests that constrain interpretation;
- within-space robustness in both systems;
- a claim that is not tied to any one frog species.

### Required framing

The manuscript must **not** be presented primarily as a frog-weather paper.

The conceptual sentence for JAE is:

> **Temporal niche separation in animal communities can be environmentally elastic: a shared short-lived cue can transiently increase behavioural overlap without detectable pairwise network rewiring.**

Frogs are the empirical system that makes this principle testable at large scale.

### Current JAE formatting requirements relevant here

- article type: Research Article;
- total word limit: 8,500 words including title page, abstract, references and legends;
- abstract: <=350 words and normally five numbered points;
- keywords: <=8;
- double-anonymized review;
- separate title page;
- explicit data-archiving statement.

The current manuscript is comfortably below the Research Article word ceiling.

## Alternative 1 — Oikos

Oikos explicitly prioritizes innovative empirical and theoretical work that advances
ecological mechanisms, processes and patterns. It is a strong alternative if the paper is
framed around **environmentally elastic temporal niche structure** rather than animal
behaviour specifically.

Reason to prefer JAE first: the response is explicitly reproductive acoustic behaviour in
animal communities, and JAE's scope directly embraces behavioural + community animal ecology.

## Alternative 2 — Ecology

Ecology is also scientifically suitable because it prioritizes concise papers documenting
important ecological phenomena and generalizations across systems. The paper would need an
especially concise presentation centered on the replicated phenomenon rather than the audit
architecture.

## Conservative fallback — Ecosphere

Ecosphere explicitly welcomes both confirmatory and innovative work across animal, climate,
freshwater and general ecology. It is a strong fallback if higher-selectivity editors judge
the rain effect too small or the prior literature too close.

## Do not target as first choice

### Ecology Letters
The replicated association is real, but the conceptual advance is currently narrower than
the relation-endpoint Paper 1 programme and is close to established rainfall-driven frog
chorusing literature. The current evidence does not justify treating this as an Ecology
Letters-level conceptual break.

### Animal Behaviour
The paper concerns behavioural community overlap, but it does not resolve individual
decision rules, social interaction mechanisms or communication consequences. The strongest
generalization is ecological rather than behavioural-mechanistic.
