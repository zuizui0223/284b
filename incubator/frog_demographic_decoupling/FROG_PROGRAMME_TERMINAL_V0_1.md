# Frog programme terminal receipt v0.1

The first standalone frog programme is **closed**.

## Primary

For *Pseudacris maculata*, the prospectively frozen `reproductive_stage × fish_present` interaction was:

- beta = +0.093
- OR = 1.10
- 95% coefficient CI = [-1.697, 1.883]
- p = 0.919

The prespecified negative directional hypothesis is not supported.

Both prespecified sensitivities agree (p = 0.601 and 0.956).

## Secondary

The frozen `reproductive_stage × year` interaction was also not supported:

- beta = -0.081
- p = 0.304

A common positive year coefficient cannot be relabelled as stage-specific decoupling.

## Frozen validation

The predeclared *Lithobates sylvaticus* validation gate fails **before any validation effect is opened**:

- eligible site-years: 20 (required 30)
- adult-positive site-years: 2 (required 5)
- reproductive-positive site-years: 10 (required 10)

Therefore no Wood Frog effect model is authorized.

## What this means

The current RMNP data do not support the intended stage-specific environmental-filtering hypothesis in the prespecified chorus-frog analysis, and the independent Wood Frog path is not estimable under the frozen gate.

This is a clean negative programme, not a failed pipeline.

## No rescue

Do not:
- switch to tiger salamanders because the frog result is null;
- search other habitat interactions for significance;
- redefine adult/reproductive stage after opening;
- call missing reproductive-stage evidence reproductive failure;
- use the shared year trend as evidence for demographic decoupling.

A future frog project must be a **new programme with a fresh hypothesis and preferably a fresh dataset or prospectively untouched outcome**, not an extension chosen to rescue this one.
