# RMNP confirmatory result v0.1

The frozen confirmatory endpoint has been opened once.

## Primary result — habitat-dependent stage filtering

Primary estimand: `reproductive_stage × fish_present`.

- beta = **+0.093**
- OR = **1.10**
- robust SE = 0.913
- 95% coefficient CI = **[-1.697, 1.883]**
- p = **0.919**

The prespecified directional hypothesis was negative. It is **not supported**.

This is not a borderline result and is not rescued by the prespecified sensitivity analyses:

- high-effort subset: beta = +0.470, p = 0.601;
- depth complete-case: beta = −0.084, p = 0.956.

Therefore the current data do **not** support the claim that fish occurrence selectively weakens reproductive-stage evidence relative to adult-stage evidence in occupied *Pseudacris maculata* site-years.

## Secondary result — temporal stage divergence

`reproductive_stage × centered_year`:

- beta = **−0.081**
- OR per year = 0.922
- 95% coefficient CI = **[-0.236, 0.074]**
- p = **0.304**

There is no evidence that reproductive-stage evidence changed through time at a detectably different rate from adult-stage evidence.

The common year coefficient is positive in the fitted models, but this is a shared stage-evidence trend and **must not be recast as demographic decoupling**.

## Boundary

The response is observed stage evidence within repeatedly surveyed site-years where the species was detected. Lack of E/L evidence is not proven reproductive failure.

No causal fish effect, recruitment, population-growth, ecological-trap or extinction-warning claim is authorized.

## Decision

This endpoint is a clean confirmatory negative. Do not retune the environmental modifier or stage definition to rescue it.

The only prospectively authorized next step is the separately frozen *Lithobates sylvaticus* validation path, after its own estimability gate.
