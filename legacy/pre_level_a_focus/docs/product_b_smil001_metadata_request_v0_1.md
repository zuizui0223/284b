# SMIL001 response-blind metadata request

## Purpose

This note prepares a metadata-only request needed to operationalize the frozen
Level-C candidate `SMIL001` without consulting focal occurrence overlap or fitted
cross-role outcomes.

The hard directional candidate is:

> successful *Smilax insularis* reproduction at a frozen reproductive opportunity
> requires adequate *Dasineura heterosmilacicola* visitation/reachability support
> at that same opportunity.

The published Current Biology 2026 article resolves the breeding-system experiment
to Amami-Oshima and early July to late September 2024, but the reviewed article
text does not publish the exact experiment coordinate. The 2025 ZooKeys paper
publishes an Amami interaction site at Higashinakama (28.2856 N, 129.4355 E), but
that coordinate must not be assumed to be the 2026 dependency-experiment site.

## Metadata questions

A response from the study authors would be used only to freeze relation-space
provenance before any focal cross-role outcome is opened.

1. What exact locality or coordinates identify the Amami-Oshima population used
   for the 2024 breeding-system exclusion/hand-pollination experiment?
2. Was that breeding-system population the same population as the Higashinakama
   site reported in Kato & Kawakita (2025), or a different population?
3. Were the approximately 75 h of Amami-Oshima pollinator observations and the
   breeding-system experiment conducted at the same population?
4. Are per-inflorescence treatment/anthesis dates available for the breeding-system
   experiment, or only the aggregate early-July to late-September 2024 interval?
5. If exact coordinates cannot be shared publicly, is there an unambiguous frozen
   locality identifier that can establish whether plant and midge answers refer to
   the same study population without disclosing sensitive coordinates?

## Information boundary

The request must not ask for or condition on:

- GBIF/iNaturalist occurrence availability;
- any fitted plant or midge suitability/reachability surface;
- any Product-B cross-role relation statistic;
- a preferred site chosen because it yields favorable overlap;
- a relaxed spatial or temporal definition after focal outcome inspection.

If the exact dependency population cannot be resolved, the exact-site Level-C
candidate remains operationally unresolved. Island-wide co-occurrence is not an
authorized substitute for the hard event relation.

This request is prepared but not sent by this repository change.
