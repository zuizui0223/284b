# Closer-antecedent novelty audit v0.1

Purpose: stress-test the Ecology Letters Method novelty claim against work closer than standard imperfect-detection papers.

## 1. Ecological model adequacy is already an explicit research programme

Getz et al. (2018), *Making ecological models adequate* (*Ecology Letters* 21:153–166; DOI 10.1111/ele.12893), argues that ecological models should be evaluated through explicit adequacy protocols, including questions about state/control variables, data determinacy, sensitivity and validity.

**Overlap with 284b:** both reject the idea that model fitting alone licenses interpretation and both treat data determinacy/adequacy as a scientific constraint.

**Remaining distinction:** the pre-field framework is not a general adequacy checklist for one ecological model. Its object is a **relation endpoint between independently generated ecological answers**. It prospectively distinguishes same-target reproducibility, soft cross-role concordance and hard event-to-function dependency; separates relation evidence from event/function answer streams; and asks whether the negative state required to contradict a hard relation is authorized by the observation process.

**Manuscript action:** cite Getz et al. early as a conceptual ancestor and avoid claiming that 284b invented model adequacy or the need to ask whether data determine a model.

## 2. Preregistration and Registered Reports already establish prospectivity

Registered Reports in ecology and evolution peer-review hypotheses, methods, sampling plans and analyses before outcome data are collected. Nature Ecology & Evolution explicitly accepts field-based and observational Registered Reports. Recent Methods in Ecology and Evolution work on adaptive preregistration likewise develops prospective transparency for model-based ecological research.

Relevant anchors:

- *Opportunities and challenges for Registered Reports in ecology and evolution* (Nature Communications, 2022).
- Nature Ecology & Evolution Registered Reports guidance and editorial rollout.
- Gould et al. (2026), *But I can't preregister my research: Improving the reproducibility and transparency of ecology and conservation with adaptive preregistration for model-based research*, *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*, DOI 10.1111/2041-210X.70311.

**Overlap with 284b:** relation, gates, candidate caps and analysis decisions are frozen before focal outcomes; post-hoc rescue is prohibited.

**Remaining distinction:** prospectivity itself is not the claimed method. Prospectivity is the enforcement mechanism for a different methodological object: choosing and authorizing the biological relation that independent ecological answers may test.

**Manuscript action:** use “prospective” descriptively, not as a novelty claim. Avoid implying that outcome-blind workflow freezing or preregistration is new.

## 3. Inference from negative evidence is an old epistemic problem

The proposition that failure to observe something is not automatically evidence of biological absence is much older than this project and extends far outside ecology.

**Overlap with 284b:** negative evidence is only meaningful under conditions where the observation process should have detected the state if present.

**Remaining distinction:** 284b operationalizes this inside a hard ecological relation endpoint. The required negative state is one component of an event-to-function implication `E(k) -> F(k)`, and invalid observation states are explicitly retained as `unresolved`. The exact benchmark then quantifies the endpoint-level cost of collapsing invalid states into negatives.

**Manuscript action:** never sell “absence of evidence is not evidence of absence” as the discovery. Sell the relation architecture, authorization gate and exact error decomposition.

## 4. Independent external validation is already established

Independent datasets and source-disjoint evaluation are established tools for testing ecological prediction, including species-distribution models.

**Overlap with 284b:** Level A uses independent specimen and human-observation sources and fresh held-out taxa.

**Remaining distinction:** Level A is an empirical anchor for a broader relation framework, not the invention of external validation. Its useful contribution is that the target, estimand, comparison frame and tolerance were frozen prospectively, and answer nonexistence remained separate from cross-source disagreement.

## 5. Detection-aware latent-state modelling is a component, not a competitor

Occupancy, co-occurrence, integrated SDM and JSDM approaches already model imperfect observation when the sampling design identifies state and detection parameters.

**Overlap with 284b:** a candidate-specific detection model could satisfy part of the negative-state identifiability requirement.

**Remaining distinction:** the proposed method sits upstream. It decides which relation two answers are entitled to test and whether the required state is identifiable enough for the endpoint to be opened. If the candidate data support a latent-state model, that model can generate a role-specific answer inside the framework. If the data do not identify detection, fitting a more elaborate latent-state model does not erase the measurement boundary.

## 6. Strongest novelty formulation after this audit

The safest high-level claim is:

> We introduce a prospective relation-endpoint architecture for independent ecological answers. The method separates same-target reproducibility from cross-role biological dependency, permits role-specific upstream estimators, maps answers to a frozen biological event space, and withholds hard falsification until the negative state required for contradiction is identifiable under the observation process.

The strongest quantitative addition is:

> For a qualified process with valid-key fraction `a`, collapsing invalid keys into biological negatives increases apparent true-violation sensitivity and false hard-violation probability by the same exact amount, `1-a`.

Neither statement requires claiming novelty for imperfect detection, external validation, preregistration or model adequacy themselves.

## 7. Editorial implication

This audit strengthens rather than weakens the Ecology Letters Method case. The method should be presented as the **composition and authorization of relations among ecological answers**, with model adequacy, detection models and preregistration treated as established ingredients that the architecture connects.

The likely desk-review question is not “is imperfect detection known?” but “is relation-endpoint authorization a sufficiently specific, generalizable research technique rather than a workflow philosophy?” The proposal figure, executable benchmark, Level-A held-out demonstration and Level-C real-system stress test should therefore remain central.
