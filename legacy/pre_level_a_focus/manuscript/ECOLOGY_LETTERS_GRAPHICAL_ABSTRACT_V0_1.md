## Short graphical-abstract text

Ecological answers from different sources or biological roles cannot be combined under one generic comparison. We introduce an executable relation-endpoint contract that freezes the biological relation, common event space, adequacy and opening rules before outcomes are viewed. Independent-answer reproducibility can close prospectively, whereas hard dependency remains unresolved until the falsifying negative state is calibrated.

## Scientific boundary

- Hard-dependency message: invalid/non-detected functional states remain unresolved unless the negative state is adequately calibrated.
- Do not show a Cremastra or Belonocnema focal biological outcome.
- Do not present 283 cells as independent replication.
