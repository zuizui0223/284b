## Figure 1. Executable relation-endpoint contract

Five objects are frozen before focal comparison: the biological relation, the biological key/event space, role-specific adapters, answer-adequacy gates and the endpoint-opening rule. A calibrated soft endpoint returns `consistent`, `attention_required` or `unresolved`; `attention_required` denotes relation discordance under the frozen rule and is not automatically biological falsification. A directional hard endpoint `E(k) -> F(k)` authorizes a hard violation only when the dependent event is adequately positive and `F(k)=false` is supported by both a qualified observation process and a focal key valid for negative inference. Levels A–C use the implemented soft/hard primitives directly; Levels D–E require relation-specific composition after their biology is frozen.

## Figure 2. Controlled Level-A cross-source empirical anchor

The same biological target was reconstructed independently from preserved-specimen and human-observation records. Twenty-four procedure-by-accessible-area reference cells were frozen before held-out opening, each using 34–47 adequate successor taxa and a nearest-rank q95 empirical source-discordance envelope. The independent held-out biological units were 12 taxa, each evaluated on a prespecified 3-area × 8-procedure diagnostic matrix. Of 288 diagnostics, 283 were evaluable and five remained unresolved at adequacy; no held-out taxon contained an envelope exceedance among its evaluable cells. Taxon-level maximum observed-discordance/envelope ratios emphasize biological replication rather than treating 283 cells as independent n. The q95 reference is an empirical envelope, not a nominal 95% predictive interval or an alpha=0.05 test.

## Figure 3. Hard dependency belongs to biological events and functions

Raw occurrence or suitability surfaces for different biological roles need not be equal or contained because persistence, mobility, observation and temporal grain can differ. A hard dependency is instead expressed at a frozen biological opportunity as `E(k) -> F(k)`, where `E(k)` is the dependent event and `F(k)` the required function at the same key. The required function can be constructed either from an externally complete provider set or through an independent aggregate functional channel. If the represented provider universe is incomplete, absence of every represented provider remains unresolved; a hard functional negative is admissible only when the aggregate function is adequately established as absent under the frozen observation design.

## Figure 4. Two real systems reach the same endpoint-openability boundary

Parallel pre-outcome audits of a pollination-function system (*Cremastra appendiculata* var. *variabilis*) and a host-resource system (*Belonocnema treatae* / live-oak budbreak). Both systems materialize separated relation evidence (`R`), dependent-event answers (`X`) and required-function/resource answers (`Y`) on biologically relevant keys with provenance separation. Both stop at the same inferential requirement: candidate-specific calibration of the negative functional state is unavailable, and identified existing raw/supplementary sources do not reconstruct it. Camera non-detection therefore does not establish absence of effective pollination, and unrecorded budbreak does not establish absence of usable resource. These lanes demonstrate endpoint openability and the need for new calibration measurement; no focal Level-C biological outcome is opened.

## Figure 5. Exact class-conditional cost of coercing invalid observation into a biological negative

For a qualified hard endpoint, let `a1=P(valid key | F=true)`, `a0=P(valid key | F=false)`, `q` be sensitivity on valid compatible keys and `sp` specificity on valid true-violation keys. Collapsing invalid observations into biological negatives adds exactly `1-a1` to false-violation probability and `1-a0` to apparent true-violation sensitivity. The earlier equality in which both increments are `1-a` is the special case `a1=a0=a`. A representative state-dependent scenario illustrates that the two costs need not be equal. A separate base-rate calculation shows that observation-process qualification is not false-discovery control: with true violation prevalence `pi=0.10`, `q=0.80` and `sp=0.99`, the false-discovery fraction among gated calls is approximately 0.645. Zero collapsing is shown as an ablation of the endpoint's unresolved-state guard, not as a competing occupancy or detection-aware model.
