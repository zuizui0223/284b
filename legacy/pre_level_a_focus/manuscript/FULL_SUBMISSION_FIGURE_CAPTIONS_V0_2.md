## Figure 1. Relation-endpoint authorization is frozen before focal opening

The relation endpoint sits above role-specific model fitting. Before focal comparison, five objects are frozen: the biological relation, the biological event/key space, role-specific adapters into that space, answer-adequacy gates and the calibration/opening rule. The complete validated contract also stores an `opening_rule_reference` identifying the frozen empirical envelope or negative-state qualification protocol that instantiates the rule. Deterministic canonical JSON and a SHA-256 fingerprint make later contract drift inspectable; fingerprinting is an audit mechanism rather than proof of outcome blindness. Calibrated soft relations return `consistent`, `attention_required` or `unresolved`. Directional hard relations `E(k) -> F(k)` authorize a hard violation only when `E(k)` is adequately positive and `F(k)=false` is supported by a qualified observation process on a focal key valid for negative inference. Inadequate, missing or unqualified negatives remain unresolved.

## Figure 2. Controlled same-target empirical anchor on 12 fresh held-out taxa

The same biological target was reconstructed independently from preserved-specimen and human-observation records. Twenty-four procedure-by-accessible-area reference cells were frozen before held-out opening, each using 34–47 adequate successor taxa and a nearest-rank q95 empirical source-discordance envelope. The independent held-out biological units were **12 taxa**, each evaluated on a prespecified 3-area × 8-procedure diagnostic matrix. Of 288 repeated diagnostics, 283 were evaluable and five remained unresolved at adequacy; **0 of 12 taxa contained an envelope exceedance among evaluable cells**. Taxon-level maxima therefore carry the replication claim, while the 283 evaluable cells provide repeated diagnostic stress tests. The q95 reference is a prospectively frozen empirical envelope, not nominal 95% predictive coverage, an alpha=0.05 test or a universal cross-role threshold.

## Figure 3. Accurate answers do not determine the biological relation they may jointly test

Panel A gives the relation-layer separation argument. For directional dependency `E -> F`, let `v=P(E=1,F=0)`. Classical Fréchet–Hoeffding coupling bounds imply `max(0,p_E-p_F) <= v <= min(p_E,1-p_F)`. Hence the same exact marginals `p_E=p_F=0.5` admit both `v=0` and `v=0.5`. The bounds are established probability theory used here only to show that improving marginal answer accuracy cannot by itself identify hard-relation status. Joint statistical models may estimate coupling, but the biological endpoint still has to declare what relation that structure is licensed to test. Panel B therefore expresses directional dependency at a frozen biological opportunity `k`, rather than through raw equality or containment of role-specific occurrence/suitability surfaces. Panel C distinguishes a genuinely complete provider set from a direct aggregate functional channel; absence in an incomplete provider universe remains unresolved.

## Figure 4. Two real systems reach the same negative-state measurement boundary

Parallel pre-outcome audits of a pollination-function system (*Cremastra appendiculata* var. *variabilis*) and a host-resource system (*Belonocnema treatae* / live-oak budbreak). Both systems materialized separated relation evidence (`R`), dependent-event answers (`X`) and required-function/resource answers (`Y`) on biologically relevant keys with provenance separation. Both then stopped before focal opening because candidate-specific calibration of the negative functional state was unavailable, and identified existing raw/supplementary sources did not reconstruct that calibration. Camera non-detection therefore does not establish absence of effective pollination, and unrecorded budbreak does not establish absence of usable resource. These lanes demonstrate the method's stopping rule and identify the next measurement requirement; no focal Level-C biological dependency outcome is opened.

## Figure 5. Exact class-specific cost of deleting the unresolved state

For a qualified hard endpoint, let `a1=P(valid key | F=true)`, `a0=P(valid key | F=false)`, `q` be sensitivity on valid compatible keys and `sp` specificity on valid true-violation keys. Coercing invalid observations into biological negatives increases false-violation probability by exactly `1-a1` and apparent sensitivity to true violations by exactly `1-a0`. Equal inflation by `1-a` is only the equal-validity special case `a1=a0=a`. Zero collapsing is shown as an ablation of the endpoint's unresolved-state guard, not as a competitor to occupancy or other detection-aware models. Observation-process qualification determines whether a hard negative is admissible; final false-discovery reliability additionally depends on the prevalence of true violations and separately frozen replication/aggregation rules.
