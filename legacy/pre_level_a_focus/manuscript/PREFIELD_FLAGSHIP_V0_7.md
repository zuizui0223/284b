# Prospective validation of ecological answers: from cross-source reproducibility to identifiable biological absence

**Reference implementation:** `scripts/relation_endpoint_contract.py`

## Abstract

Ecological answers are increasingly combined across observation sources and biological roles, yet reproducibility and dependency require different evidence. We introduce an executable relation-endpoint contract that freezes the relation, biological key space, role-specific adapters, adequacy gates and opening rule before focal outcomes are viewed. Twelve fresh held-out taxa generated 288 prespecified same-target cell diagnostics; 283 were evaluable and all remained within frozen empirical source-discordance envelopes, while five stayed unresolved. For hard dependency `E(k) -> F(k)`, the negative state must itself be identifiable. An exact decomposition shows that collapsing invalid observations into biological negatives inflates false violations by `1-a1` and apparent violation sensitivity by `1-a0`, where validity may differ by latent function state; the previous `1-a` identity is the equal-validity case. Two prospective cross-role systems remained sealed at the same measurement boundary. The method separates answer reproducibility from biological dependency and identifies when new measurement is required.

## 1. Introduction

Ecological synthesis increasingly combines answers generated by different observation processes. Species distributions are reconstructed from museum specimens, opportunistic observations, structured surveys and sensors; interacting taxa are represented by different streams; and host, pollinator, consumer and life-stage responses are estimated at different spatial and temporal grains. Independent data can strengthen external evaluation, while specimen and observation records can differ substantially in sampling process (Matutini et al. 2021; Erickson & Smith 2021; Gaier & Resasco 2023). The inferential problem is therefore not only whether multiple answers can be constructed, but what relation those answers are scientifically entitled to test.

The ingredients are established. Occupancy models separate latent ecological state from observation (MacKenzie et al. 2004; Guillera-Arroita 2017), multispecies models accommodate co-occurrence under imperfect detection (Rota et al. 2016), and interaction work distinguishes missed interactions from non-occurrence (Weinstein & Graham 2017). Getz et al. (2018) made model adequacy and data determinacy explicit methodological concerns. Chadwick et al. (2024) systematized observation-process problems through LIES—Latency, Identifiability, Effort and Scale. Preregistration and Registered Reports separately establish outcome-blind design. We therefore do **not** claim novelty for imperfect detection, observation-process identifiability, model adequacy, external validation or prospectivity.

Our object is the **relation endpoint between independently generated ecological answers**. Two reconstructions of the same target can be compared as a controlled cross-source problem because target and estimand can be held fixed. Different biological roles generally cannot be required to have identical suitability surfaces, fitted values or accessible areas. A perennial plant can persist after local pollinator loss and an adult life stage can occur outside larval habitat. Cross-role inference instead requires a relation defined at the biological event where dependency applies.

Hard dependency adds a further requirement. If a dependent event `E(k)` requires a biological function `F(k)` at opportunity `k`, then `E(k)=true` with `F(k)=false` can contradict the dependency. But an observational zero can also result from incomplete coverage, imperfect detection, missingness or failure. Detection-aware models can estimate latent state when sampling identifies the observation process; when it does not, a hard contradiction cannot acquire its negative premise merely through a more elaborate fit.

We develop an estimator-agnostic relation-endpoint method with three separable tasks: construct adequate role-specific answers, project them into a biological relation space, and authorize only those endpoint states identified by the observation process. We demonstrate the controlled same-target layer on fresh held-out taxa, derive exact consequences of an invalid-state coercion ablation, and use prospective cross-role audits to locate the point at which existing data cease to identify a hard endpoint.

## 2. Relation-endpoint architecture

### 2.1 Five-part executable endpoint contract

Before focal comparison values are opened, the method freezes a five-part **relation-endpoint contract**:

1. the biological relation to be tested;
2. the biological event or key space in which it applies;
3. adapters mapping each role-specific answer into that space;
4. answer-adequacy gates determining whether an answer exists;
5. a calibration/opening rule distinguishing admissible relation evidence from unresolved observation.

The contract is estimator-agnostic. An answer can come from an occupancy model, SDM, phenology model, sensor classifier or direct measurement. The endpoint contract does not replace estimator assumptions; it determines what joint relation their outputs may test.

The reference implementation, `scripts/relation_endpoint_contract.py`, turns the contract into an endpoint state machine. A calibrated soft key returns `consistent`, `attention_required` or `unresolved`. A directional hard key returns `hard_violation_authorized`, `no_observed_violation`, `noninformative_for_implication` or `unresolved`. Inadequate answers, missing comparison values and unqualified negatives are structurally unable to become biological contradictions.

We distinguish **Level A**, same target reconstructed from independent observation sources; **Level B**, different roles expected to show soft concordance; **Level C**, directional dependency; **Level D**, mutual dependency; and **Level E**, life-stage coupling. The ladder ranks required inferential structure rather than ecological importance. The reference implementation directly covers the Level-A soft relation and Level-C hard implication demonstrated here; the other levels require their own frozen biological relation and can be composed from the same contract primitives.

At Level A, holding target, estimand, estimator family, accessible-area semantics and comparison rows fixed is a deliberate control. For Levels B–E, forcing common estimators, predictors, accessible areas or raw scales can be biologically wrong. Each role is therefore constructed defensibly first and then adapted to a common relation space, such as plant site × flowering window or host patch × developmental interval.

### 2.2 Soft coherence and hard dependency

Soft relations quantify discordance after adaptation and require relation-specific calibration. A same-target source-discordance distribution is not a universal tolerance for different roles.

For directional hard dependency we write

`E(k) -> F(k)`,

where `E(k)` is the dependent event and `F(k)` the required function at the same opportunity key. A hard violation requires an adequately positive `E(k)` and an adequately negative `F(k)`.

The required side is defined at the **function** level when several providers can satisfy the same requirement. Effective pollination, compatible host resource or accessible prey need not map to one provider species. Provider occurrence or suitability is not automatically the function, and an incomplete provider set cannot establish functional absence merely because all represented providers are absent.

## 3. Methods

### 3.1 Controlled same-target cross-source experiment

We constructed independent ecological answers from preserved-specimen and human-observation records. Within each procedure-by-accessible-area cell, target identity, ecological estimand, `bio1-bio19` predictor universe, estimator/procedure identity, accessible-area semantics, an exact 2,000-row comparison frame, adequacy logic and successor-derived reference rule were fixed.

The held-out outcome was not used to select procedures, accessible areas, taxa or rescue paths. A successor audit sealed 2,255/2,256 fit cells and retained the unresolved cell. Each reference cell required at least 30 prospectively adequate successor taxa. All 24 procedure-by-accessible-area cells met that rule, with 34–47 distinct adequate successor taxa per cell, and were frozen with the unchanged nearest-rank q95 rule before held-out paired discordance was opened.

The q95 value is used here as a **prospectively frozen empirical source-discordance envelope**. It is not interpreted as a nominal 95% predictive interval, an `alpha=0.05` hypothesis test or family-wise error control across the 24 reference cells.

The independent held-out biological units were 12 taxa. Each taxon contributed a prespecified 3 accessible-area × 8 procedure matrix, giving 288 cell diagnostics. A cell entered the cross-source check only if both answers passed predeclared adequacy. Thus the cell diagnostics are repeated within taxon and are not treated as 288 independent replicates. Discordance was `1 - Schoener D` relative to the frozen empirical reference ceiling.

### 3.2 Prospective Level-C openability audit

Directional dependency required three prospectively separated evidence channels: external evidence defining the biological relation (`R`), an independent dependent-event stream (`X`) and an independent required-function stream (`Y`). Independence was defined by observation generation rather than publication label.

Candidate discovery was finite and response-blind. Function-level semantics replaced unjustified single-provider exclusivity; subsequent gates required a common event key, direct role estimands and provenance separation. Unavailable sources, failed downloads, missing records and uncalibrated zeros were never permitted to count as biological absence.

The retained systems are used in Paper 1 only as **pre-outcome audits of endpoint openability**. No focal Level-C cross-role value, hard invariant or biological dependency outcome is part of the present empirical claim.

### 3.3 Negative-state identifiability and v8.1 repair

For hard endpoints, we distinguish detected function, calibrated functional absence, unresolved non-detection, and observation failure/missingness. Only calibrated absence can supply `F(k)=false`.

The prospective calibration floor requires key-level sensitivity >=0.80, hard false-negative rate <=0.20 and specificity >=0.95, with explicit quality control and missingness separation. A pass authorizes only a later endpoint-opening stage.

Before any field-calibration data were entered, audit of the frozen v8 operational package found that the contract required a one-sided 95% exact-or-more-conservative confidence rule while the evaluator implemented a Wilson lower bound. v8 was retained as immutable history and v8.1 was prospectively frozen using one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson bounds, with candidate identities, thresholds, minimum counts and missingness rules unchanged.

### 3.4 Exact invalid-state coercion ablation

We compare two endpoint rules on synthetic keys with known latent truth. The **zero-collapsing ablation** deliberately violates the contract by treating invalid observation or a valid non-detection as `F=false` when `E=true`. It is not presented as a competitor to occupancy modelling, LIES or other detection-aware methods. Its purpose is to quantify the decision cost of the forbidden operation. The **identifiability-gated rule** permits a hard negative only when the observation process qualifies and the key is complete, nonmissing and nonfailed; otherwise the zero is unresolved.

Let `r` be the true functional-event probability per opportunity, `s` detection probability for a true event and `n` the number of opportunities. Conditional on at least one true event in the key, key-level sensitivity is

`q = [1 - (1 - r s)^n] / [1 - (1 - r)^n]`.

To allow observation validity to depend on latent function state, define

`a1 = P(valid key | F=true)`

for biologically compatible keys and

`a0 = P(valid key | F=false)`

for true violations. For `F=true`,

`FPR_zero = 1 - a1 q`

and

`FPR_gated = a1(1-q)`,

so

`FPR_zero - FPR_gated = 1-a1`.

For a true violation with key-level specificity `sp`,

`TPR_zero = 1 - a0(1-sp)`

and

`TPR_gated = a0 sp`,

so

`TPR_zero - TPR_gated = 1-a0`.

Thus invalid-state coercion contributes an exact class-specific error increment. The previously reported identity in which both increments equal `1-a` is the controlled special case `a1=a0=a`.

The original 8,748-scenario grid applied the same complete-window, missingness and failure process to both latent function states and therefore evaluates this equal-validity corollary. A separate outcome-blind audit (`pre_field_state_dependent_invalidity_v0_2`) verifies the generalized decomposition without reading focal Level-C values.

For a qualified process on valid keys, the false-discovery fraction among called violations is

`FDF = (1-pi)(1-q) / [(1-pi)(1-q) + pi sp]`,

where `pi` is true violation prevalence. This separates process qualification from endpoint-level evidentiary reliability.

### 3.5 Calibration operating characteristics

We evaluated the fixed v8.1 calibration decision using exact binomial probabilities before field data. At the minimum sample sizes, a one-sided 95% exact lower bound above the thresholds requires at least 28/30 known-positive sensitivity successes and 60/60 known-negative specificity successes. These are outcome-blind design diagnostics, not candidate-specific estimates, and assume independent calibration units.

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Fresh Level-A closure

The 12 prospectively held-out taxa generated 288 prespecified cell diagnostics. Both answers passed adequacy in 283 cells; five stayed closed. Each taxon contributed 22–24 evaluable cells. No held-out taxon contained a ceiling exceedance: all 12 taxa had all of their evaluable cell diagnostics within the corresponding frozen empirical source-discordance envelope.

The **283/283** count is therefore a cell-level diagnostic summary, not an independent sample size. We make no binomial success-probability claim from 283 repeated cells.

The closest opened cell was *Nothofagus betuloides* under the 150-km accessible area and `predictive_forward|logit_l2_C1_degree2`: discordance was 0.38625 versus a frozen ceiling of 0.39085, a margin of 0.00460. Only one of 283 evaluable cells lay within 0.025 of its ceiling and six lay within 0.05. The result supports same-target cross-source reproducibility **conditional on answer adequacy and relative to the predeclared empirical envelopes**, not exact source invariance or nominal 95% coverage.

### 4.2 Real systems reach the same measurement boundary

Finite response-blind screening retained two biologically different systems with separated relation, event and function evidence streams. `CREMV3-007` (*Cremastra appendiculata* var. *variabilis*) combined external breeding-system evidence, same-inflorescence fruit outcome and pollinia-carrying camera observations. `BELV3-012` (*Belonocnema treatae* / live-oak budbreak) combined independent host-specificity evidence, natural adult emergence and live-oak budbreak observations.

Both passed source materialization, common-key alignment, direct event/function estimands, provenance separation and no-post-hoc-redefinition gates. Both stopped before focal opening because functional absence was not independently calibrated. Positive pollinia-carrying contacts are directly informative for Cremastra, but camera non-detection is not validated as absence of effective pollination. Belonocnema emergence and budbreak are separately observed, but an unrecorded usable-resource state cannot yet be distinguished from incomplete observation.

Candidate-specific calibration audits found no independent stream sufficient to estimate required sensitivity and specificity without conditioning on focal outcomes. Identified supplementary/raw sources also failed to reconstruct such calibration. These are measurement-boundary results, not biological negatives. The next admissible information is prospective measurement.

### 4.3 Invalid-state coercion has exact class-specific costs

For any qualified process under the generalized decomposition,

`FPR_zero - FPR_gated = 1-a1`

and

`TPR_zero - TPR_gated = 1-a0`.

The increments need not be equal when observation validity depends on latent function state. For example, with `a1=0.70`, `a0=0.90`, `q=0.95` and `sp=0.99`, false-violation probability increases from 0.035 under the gated rule to 0.335 under zero collapsing, an increment of 0.30. Apparent true-violation sensitivity increases from 0.891 to 0.991, an increment of 0.10.

The original 8,748-scenario equal-validity grid contained 4,320 calibration-passing and 4,428 calibration-failing combinations. Across every passing combination, both increments equalled `1-a` to floating-point error below `1.2 x 10^-16`. Under the frozen `q >= 0.80` floor, gated key-level false-violation probability was never greater than 0.20 across that grid. Zero-collapsing false-violation probability reached 0.776 because invalid keys were counted as biological negatives.

In the original mixed qualified regime with `q=0.8653`, `sp=0.95` and `a=0.684`, zero-collapsing false-violation probability was 0.4081 and gated probability 0.0921. Their difference, 0.316, equalled the common invalid-key mass; apparent true-violation sensitivity increased by the same 0.316 because that scenario imposed `a1=a0`.

When calibration failed, the gated rule made no hard calls. This ablation therefore quantifies the value of preserving unresolved states; it does not claim superiority over detection-aware models that already identify and model the observation process.

### 4.4 Calibration is not false-discovery control

The gate controls whether a negative state is eligible to enter a hard endpoint; it does not guarantee that a called violation is probably a true violation. With `pi=0.10`, `q=0.80` and `sp=0.99`, for example, the false-discovery fraction among gated calls is approximately 0.645 even with complete valid-key observation.

Calibration is therefore an endpoint-opening condition. Replication, aggregation or posterior evidence rules must be specified separately before focal Level-C values are opened.

### 4.5 Minimum calibration counts are feasibility minima

At the v8.1 minimum, 30 known-positive units require at least 28 successes. If true sensitivity is 0.90, nominal pass probability is 0.411; at 0.95 it is 0.812. Sixty known-negative units require 60/60 specificity successes; pass probability at true specificity 0.99 is `0.99^60 = 0.547`, increasing to 0.942 at specificity 0.999.

These calculations do not authorize adaptive sample-size extension. Any larger design must be chosen prospectively rather than used as rescue after observed failure.

## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 The method operates above observation-model choice

LIES is the closest conceptual antecedent: Chadwick et al. (2024) explicitly systematize observation-process identifiability. Occupancy models estimate latent state under informative sampling, interaction models distinguish missed interactions from non-occurrence, and adequacy frameworks ask whether data support an intended model (Getz et al. 2018). The relation-endpoint method can contain any of these components.

Its additional object is **relation choice and endpoint authorization across independently generated answers**. Statistical co-occurrence, predictive similarity and hard biological dependency are different claims. A well-characterized observation process does not determine which relation should hold between two role-specific answers. The executable endpoint contract fixes the common biological event and the rule by which individually defensible answers may compose into a joint claim.

Prospectivity prevents the endpoint from being redefined after a failure is observed, but prospectivity itself is not the novelty claim. The narrower contribution is the five-part contract, explicit unresolved state and relation-specific opening logic.

### 5.2 Level A is a controlled empirical anchor, not 283 independent replications

Level A demonstrates that a predeclared relation between independent reconstructions can close empirically without using held-out outcomes to tune procedure, accessible area or envelope. The biological replication is the 12 held-out taxa. Repeated procedure and accessible-area cells probe the stability of the declared relation under a prespecified analysis matrix but are correlated within taxon.

Likewise, nearest-rank q95 is used as a predeclared empirical envelope, not a nominal prediction interval. The claim is therefore deliberately conditional: on fresh held-out taxa, every evaluable cell diagnostic fell within the successor-derived envelope appropriate to that procedure and accessible-area setting. This is an empirical anchor for endpoint authorization, not a universal source-invariance theorem.

### 5.3 The ablation isolates a forbidden operation rather than competing with occupancy models

The zero-collapsing rule is intentionally not a state-of-the-art comparator. It removes one guardrail from the endpoint contract—preservation of invalid observations as unresolved—and measures the exact decision consequence. Detection-aware occupancy, interaction or sensor models can satisfy the negative-state gate when their sampling design identifies the relevant state.

The generalized decomposition also makes the scope transparent. Equal inflation of false violations and apparent sensitivity occurs only under equal class-conditional validity. When validity is state-dependent, each increment tracks its own invalid-state mass. The useful result is therefore the decomposition and authorization logic, not mathematical difficulty.

### 5.4 A scientific stopping rule

The Level-C systems illustrate the stopping rule. Once relation definition, event-key alignment, evidence independence and positive estimands are adequate, but the negative function state remains indistinguishable from non-observation, additional fitting of the same data cannot complete the hard inference unless the sampling design contains information that identifies that state.

This is not unfinished biological evidence. No Level-C dependency outcome is claimed in Paper 1. The systems are pre-outcome stress tests showing that the method can identify a concrete measurement requirement rather than manufacture a negative state.

### 5.5 Scope and limitations

The contract does not guarantee that the chosen biological relation is true; external biology must justify it. It does not repair misspecified upstream models; their adequacy remains their own problem. Calibration thresholds are scientific design choices rather than universal constants. The current reference implementation directly covers calibrated soft coherence and directional hard implication; mutual dependency and stage-coupling endpoints require relation-specific composition and validation.

Finally, endpoint qualification and final biological evidence are separate. A qualified negative can enter a hard endpoint, but base rates, replication and aggregation determine how strongly one or more calls support a scientific conclusion.

### 5.6 Generality

The architecture extends beyond the motivating systems. Host-dependent development requires a compatible resource during a developmental opportunity, not generic host occurrence. Trophic dependency concerns accessible prey at a relevant foraging opportunity, not equality of predator and prey distributions. Life-stage coupling concerns transition-compatible habitat at a transition event. Sensor-based interaction studies require calibrated observation before non-observation can act as a hard negative.

Across cases, the procedure is the same: construct role-appropriate answers independently, map them to the relation where biology makes a prediction, preserve unresolved states, and verify that the state needed to contradict the relation is identifiable before allowing hard inference.

## 6. Conclusion

Reproducibility, soft biological concordance and hard dependency are different ecological relation endpoints. A controlled same-target relation can be evaluated prospectively, as the 12-taxon Level-A result demonstrates. Hard cross-role inference requires the state that would falsify the dependency to be observable and calibrated.

The generalized exact decomposition shows why unresolved states matter: coercing invalid observation into a biological negative adds the compatible-class invalid mass to false violations and the violation-class invalid mass to apparent sensitivity. The equal `1-a` identity is the class-independent special case. Calibration determines whether a hard negative is admissible; it does not guarantee the evidentiary reliability of a called violation.

When negative-state identifiability is absent, the next scientific step is not automatically a more elaborate model of the same observations. It is a measurement design that can identify the missing state.
