# Pre-field Paper 1 submission cascade v0.1

Purpose: freeze the journal-routing logic before editorial outcomes are known. This prevents post hoc reframing of the scientific claim after a journal decision.

## Route 1 — Ecology Letters, Method

**Status:** primary / highest justified pre-field target.

Why it fits:
- the article introduces a specific, generalizable research technique rather than a taxon-specific result;
- statistical performance is characterized analytically and over 8,748 exact benchmark scenarios;
- code and frozen tests are available;
- a completed fresh Level-A held-out case study demonstrates the method;
- Level-C systems provide a real-world stress test without being presented as completed biological endpoints.

**Canonical novelty statement:** relation-endpoint authorization across independently generated ecological answers.

**Do not change for Ecology Letters:** Level-A empirical result, Level-B unopened status, Level-C focal closure, exact benchmark identity, empirical ledger, thresholds or candidate set.

### If invited

Use `PREFIELD_FLAGSHIP_V0_6.md` as the full-manuscript base. Complete only human/title-page formatting, final references, figures, archival release and journal-specific declarations.

### If proposal is declined as too conceptual / not sufficiently a Method

Do not add focal Level-C data to rescue Paper 1. Route to The American Naturalist as a Major Article unless the editor's response points specifically to a methodological deficiency better addressed at MEE.

## Route 2 — The American Naturalist, Major Article

**Trigger:** Ecology Letters declines because the contribution is judged primarily conceptual rather than a sufficiently new technique.

The American Naturalist explicitly considers novel methods when they advance a general conceptual issue, are illustrated on data and have broader use. This matches the strongest conceptual reading of the project.

### Reframing allowed

Lead with the general conceptual question:

> When can independently generated ecological answers legitimately support a stronger biological relation than the answers establish individually?

Structure the paper around the hierarchy:

1. answer observability and adequacy;
2. relation-space compatibility;
3. cross-validity;
4. hard dependency and negative-state identifiability;
5. the stopping boundary between modelling and measurement.

The exact `1-a` result becomes a formal consequence of preserving unresolved states rather than the primary Method-performance pitch.

### Reframing forbidden

- do not imply Level C is empirically confirmed or falsified;
- do not import Level-A q95 to cross-role endpoints;
- do not reopen candidate hunting;
- do not tune thresholds from editorial feedback;
- do not describe observational unavailability as a biological result.

---

## Route 3 — Methods in Ecology and Evolution, Research Article

**Trigger:** Ecology Letters declines for breadth/priority, or The American Naturalist declines because the contribution is better treated as methodology than general conceptual ecology.

MEE explicitly accepts analytical, practical and conceptual methodological developments. Its Research Article guidance expects simulations or benchmark data for computational methods and broad applicability across taxa/systems. The current exact benchmark and cross-system architecture satisfy that direction better than a Workflow framing.

### Article type

Prefer **Research Article**, not Workflow.

Reason: the contribution is not primarily an assemblage of existing software into a Big-data pipeline. The new object is the relation-endpoint authorization method plus its quantitative decision properties. Current MEE guidance notes that descriptions of workflows linking existing methods generally are not considered new methods, while Research Articles should describe and test a genuinely new method.

### Strengthening allowed before MEE submission

- expand transfer examples across pollination, host dependence, trophic interactions and life-stage coupling;
- provide executable API/tutorial documentation for the five-part contract;
- expand benchmark sensitivity analysis without reading focal Level-C values;
- add comparison examples showing how occupancy/LIES/model-adequacy components plug into the endpoint contract.

### Strengthening forbidden

The same scientific boundaries listed above remain frozen.

---

## Journal-independent invariant

Editorial routing may change exposition but not scientific truth. Across all three routes:

- Level A = one completed empirical conclusion;
- Level B = unopened;
- Level C = prospective architecture/calibration boundary, not a biological endpoint;
- empirical ledger = 1;
- unavailable/missing/non-detection without adequate calibration != biological negative;
- focal Level-C values remain sealed until separately authorized.

## Decision rule

1. Send Ecology Letters Method proposal first.
2. If invited, submit there.
3. If declined as primarily conceptual, route to The American Naturalist Major Article.
4. If declined for priority/breadth or AN judges it primarily methodological, route to MEE Research Article.
5. Never use a journal rejection as authorization to change the frozen scientific endpoint.
