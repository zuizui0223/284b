# Table 1. Relation-endpoint claim/evidence matrix

This is a manuscript display item. It summarizes inferential status without opening any new focal Level-C biological values.

| Inferential level | Relation / object | Evidence required | Completed evidence | Current status | Allowed claim | Prohibited claim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level A — answer construction | Same target reconstructed independently from specimen and human-observation sources | Both source-specific answers pass frozen adequacy gates on prespecified held-out units | 12 fresh held-out taxa; 288 prespecified procedure×area diagnostics; 283 evaluable; 5 unresolved | **Closed empirically for admitted cells** | Independent answers can be constructed and checked when both are adequate | Five unresolved cells were failures; 283 cells are independent replicates |
| Level A — cross-validity | Same-target source-discordance relative to frozen reference envelope | Prospectively frozen relation-specific empirical reference | 24/24 reference cells frozen; 34–47 adequate successor taxa per cell; 0/12 held-out taxa with any envelope exceedance among evaluable cells | **Empirically supported** | Same-target cross-source reproducibility conditional on answer adequacy | q95 is a nominal 95% predictive interval; q95 is a universal cross-role threshold |

## Notes

- Level-A replication is by **12 held-out taxa**. The 283 evaluable procedure×area cells are repeated diagnostics, not independent n.
- The nearest-rank q95 reference is a **prospectively frozen empirical source-discordance envelope**, not a nominal predictive interval or hypothesis-test threshold.
- For the hard-endpoint ablation, use the generalized notation `a1=P(valid|F=true)` and `a0=P(valid|F=false)`; the old `1-a` equality is only the equal-validity special case.
- Calibration qualification is an endpoint-opening condition, not proof that an individual called violation is biologically true.
- Empirical ledger remains **1** throughout this pre-field paper.
