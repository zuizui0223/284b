# Ecology Letters editor novelty reply v1

**Use only if the editors ask how the Method differs from adjacent frameworks.** This note is not part of the frozen Stage-1 package and does not alter any empirical endpoint or submission identity.

## Short reply

The proposed Method is not a replacement for occupancy models, JSDMs, causal DAGs, data fusion or preregistration. Those frameworks solve upstream problems: estimating latent ecological state, estimating joint statistical structure, identifying causal effects under explicit assumptions, combining heterogeneous evidence, or protecting analytic decisions from outcome-dependent revision. Our target is the logically subsequent **composition problem**: once ecological answers are individually defensible, which biological relation are those answers licensed to test together, on which biological opportunity/key, and what observation state is sufficient to open or contradict that relation?

The distinction matters because a statistically valid answer can still be composed into a biologically invalid claim. A JSDM can estimate coupling but does not by itself decide whether the relevant scientific endpoint is co-occurrence, directional dependency, mutual dependency, stage transition or effective biological function. A DAG can encode and identify causal structure but the present Method does not claim causal-effect identification and also covers non-causal same-target and compatibility relations. Preregistration can freeze an analysis prospectively but does not determine whether the frozen comparison represents the right biological relation. Relation-endpoint authorization is therefore designed to consume outputs from these established methods, not displace them.

## One-sentence version

> Existing methods govern answer construction, observation correction, joint estimation, causal identification and prospective analysis control; relation endpoints govern the downstream authorization of what multiple defensible answers may jointly claim biologically.

## Empirical and analytic support carried by the paper

- fresh held-out closure across 12 independent taxa under a frozen same-target relation;
- a classical coupling counterexample showing that exact marginals do not identify a hard directional relation;
- an exact `1-a1` / `1-a0` cost for deleting the unresolved state;
- two real-system openability audits that stop at the same uncalibrated negative-state boundary rather than manufacture biological negatives.
