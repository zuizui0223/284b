## Short graphical-abstract text — 431 characters including spaces

Ecological answers from different sources or biological roles cannot be combined under one generic comparison. We introduce an executable relation-endpoint contract that freezes the biological relation, common event space, adequacy and opening rules before outcomes are viewed. Independent-answer reproducibility can close prospectively, whereas hard dependency remains unresolved until the falsifying negative state is calibrated.

## Scientific boundary

- Level-A message: independent-answer reproducibility can be evaluated under a prospectively frozen empirical relation.
- The graphic uses **12 held-out taxa / 0 of 12 taxa with an empirical-envelope exceedance**, not 283 cells as independent replication.
- Hard-dependency message: invalid or missing observation is not biological absence; an unqualified negative remains unresolved.
- Do not show a Cremastra or Belonocnema focal biological outcome.
- Do not present q95 as nominal predictive coverage.
- Empirical ledger remains 1.
