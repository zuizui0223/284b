# Ecology Letters invited Method — cover letter v1

Dear Editors,

Please consider our invited Method manuscript, **“Relation endpoints for ecological inference: when independent answers can support joint biological claims,”** for publication in *Ecology Letters*.

Ecological analyses increasingly combine answers produced by different observation processes, but individually defensible answers do not automatically authorize a joint biological claim. We introduce a relation-endpoint framework that prospectively freezes the relation being tested, the common biological key, answer-adequacy requirements, and the rule that determines whether a joint endpoint is opened or remains unresolved.

The manuscript is anchored by two completed positive results. First, in a prospectively held-out empirical test, independent species-distribution answers from preserved-specimen and human-observation records were compared across 12 plant taxa under 24 prespecified procedure-by-area diagnostics per taxon. Of 288 diagnostics, 283 were evaluable and five remained unresolved; **none of the 12 held-out taxa contained an exceedance of its prospectively frozen source-discordance envelope**. The independent biological replication is the 12 taxa, not the repeated diagnostic cells.

Second, we derive the exact consequence of coercing an invalid observation key into a biological zero. For a hard endpoint, the false-violation increment is exactly `1-a1`, while the apparent true-violation sensitivity increment is `1-a0`; when validity is class-independent both reduce to `1-a`. We further show that passing an observation-validity gate does not itself control endpoint false-discovery fraction. These results give a quantitative reason to preserve unresolved states without turning the framework into a mechanism for avoiding positive conclusions.

The paper is intentionally narrower than earlier development versions. It does **not** rely on unresolved Level-C candidate systems, failed biological searches, or unopened dependency endpoints. The methodological argument is complete with one positive held-out ecological endpoint and the exact invalid-state analysis.

We position the contribution as an inferential authorization layer that complements, rather than replaces, occupancy and detection models, joint species-distribution models, data fusion, model adequacy, observation-process frameworks, external validation, and preregistration. The method is designed to make explicit which biological relation independently generated answers are licensed to support and when missing evidentiary states must remain unresolved.

The manuscript is within the invited Method limits, uses four planned main display items, and is accompanied by tested code, frozen endpoint contracts, machine-readable empirical receipts, and reproducible mathematical audits.
