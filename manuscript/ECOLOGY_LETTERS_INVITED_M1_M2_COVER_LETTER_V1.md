# Ecology Letters invited Method — cover letter v1

Dear Editors,

Please consider our invited Method manuscript, **“Relation endpoints for ecological inference: when independent answers can support joint biological claims,”** for publication in *Ecology Letters*.

Ecological analyses increasingly combine answers produced by different observation processes, but individually defensible answers do not automatically authorize a joint biological claim. We introduce a relation-endpoint framework that prospectively freezes the relation being tested, the common biological key, answer-adequacy requirements, and the rule that determines whether a joint endpoint is opened or remains unresolved.

The manuscript is anchored by two completed contributions: a held-out ecological test and an exact structural result. First, in a prospectively held-out empirical test, independent species-distribution answers from preserved-specimen and human-observation records were compared across 12 plant taxa under 24 prespecified procedure-by-area diagnostics per taxon. Of 288 diagnostics, 283 were evaluable and five remained unresolved; **none of the 12 held-out taxa contained an exceedance of its prospectively frozen source-discordance envelope**. The independent biological replication is the 12 taxa, not the repeated diagnostic cells.

We additionally report a complete, separately signed **retrospective source-completeness sensitivity audit** prompted by restoration of 510 GBIF Parquet objects that were missing from the nominal original snapshot. All 59 prespecified taxa were refitted under their original spatial geometry and procedures, all 24 reference q95 envelopes were recalibrated independently of the held-out panel, and **no held-out taxon exceeded the revised envelope** among 285 evaluable comparisons. Crucially, one of the **283 originally evaluable comparisons became unresolved**, while three previously unresolved comparisons became evaluable. We therefore **do not** claim full cell-coverage robustness: the stricter source-completeness sensitivity criterion failed despite the repeated taxon-level 0/12 non-exceedance pattern. The original empirical endpoint is preserved, and the repair is not counted as independent biological replication.

Second, we derive the exact consequence of coercing an invalid observation key into a biological zero. For a hard endpoint, the false-violation increment is exactly `1-a1`, while the apparent true-violation sensitivity increment is `1-a0`; when validity is class-independent both reduce to `1-a`. We further show that passing an observation-validity gate does not itself control endpoint false-discovery fraction: when validity differs by latent biological state, the prevalence among qualified keys and hence the false-discovery fraction change, even if the conditional detection properties are fixed. The previously stated validity-cancelling FDF expression is explicitly restricted to equal positive validity (or prevalence defined among valid keys). These results give a quantitative reason to preserve unresolved states without turning the framework into a mechanism for avoiding positive conclusions.

The paper is intentionally narrower than earlier development versions. It does **not** rely on unresolved Level-C candidate systems, failed biological searches, or unopened dependency endpoints. The methodological argument rests on one historical held-out ecological endpoint and the exact invalid-state analysis; the later source-repair check is presented transparently as a coverage-limited sensitivity result rather than new ecological evidence.

We position the contribution as an inferential authorization layer that complements, rather than replaces, occupancy and detection models, joint species-distribution models, data fusion, model adequacy, observation-process frameworks, external validation, and preregistration. The method is designed to make explicit which biological relation independently generated answers are licensed to support and when missing evidentiary states must remain unresolved.

The manuscript uses four planned main display items and is accompanied by tested code, frozen endpoint contracts, original and repaired-source machine-readable receipts, and reproducible mathematical audits. Final journal formatting, figure rendering, author approval and a permanent versioned archive DOI remain outstanding before submission.
