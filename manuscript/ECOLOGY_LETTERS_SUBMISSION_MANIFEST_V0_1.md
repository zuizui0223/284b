## Submission constraints enforced by repository tests

- Method abstract <=150 words;
- Method main text <=5,000 words;
- unsolicited proposal <=300 words;
- exactly 6 planned display items;
- explicit prior-work positioning against occupancy/detection modelling, LIES, model adequacy and preregistration;
- explicit five-part relation-endpoint contract;
- no language claiming Level-C dependency confirmation/falsification;
- no focal Level-C values read by the synthetic benchmark;
- v8.1 remains pre-data and nonempirical.
