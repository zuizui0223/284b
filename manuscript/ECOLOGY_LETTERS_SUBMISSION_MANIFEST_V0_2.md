## Submission constraints enforced by repository tests

- Method abstract <=150 words;
- Method main text <=5,000 words;
- unsolicited proposal <=300 words;
- exactly 6 planned display items;
- independent held-out unit stated as 12 taxa, with 283 cells explicitly non-independent diagnostics;
- nearest-rank q95 described as an empirical envelope rather than nominal predictive coverage or a hypothesis test;
- invalid-state result stated in generalized `a1/a0` form, with `1-a` only as equal-validity corollary;
- zero collapsing described as an ablation/forbidden operation rather than a modern competing method;
- executable five-part relation-endpoint contract present and tested;
- no language claiming Level-C dependency confirmation/falsification;
- no focal Level-C values read by synthetic audits;
- v8.1 remains pre-data and nonempirical.

## Scientific claim boundary

- **Level A:** 12 fresh held-out taxa; 288 prespecified cell diagnostics; 283 evaluable cells within prospectively frozen empirical source-discordance envelopes; five unresolved. The 283 cells are not independent replicates.
- **Level B:** unopened.
- **Level C:** two retained architecture-qualified systems used only as pre-outcome endpoint-openability audits; focal cross-role values, hard invariant, soft cross-check and process knockout remain sealed pending prospective calibration.
