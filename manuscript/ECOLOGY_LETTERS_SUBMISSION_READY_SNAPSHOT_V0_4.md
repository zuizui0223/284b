## Relation-layer separation

Exact marginal answers do not generally identify a hard directional relation. At `p_E=p_F=0.5`, classical Fréchet-Hoeffding coupling bounds permit `P(E=1,F=0)` from 0 to 0.5 under different joint couplings. This is established probability theory used as a separation argument, not claimed as new mathematics.

JSDMs are explicitly treated as antecedent/upstream joint-modelling tools that may estimate coupling. The retained novelty is the biological authorization layer that specifies which relation that joint structure is licensed to test and what evidence may open or contradict it.
