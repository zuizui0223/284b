## Claims prohibited in the short report

1. 283/283 independent successes.
2. A binomial success probability based on 283 cells.
3. Universal source invariance.
4. Preserved specimens and human observations have identical observation processes.
5. The nearest-rank q95 envelope is a nominal 95% prediction interval.
6. alpha = 0.05 hypothesis-test language for the envelope.
7. One modelling procedure or accessible-area radius is superior based on the held-out result.
8. Any Level-B or Level-C biological dependency conclusion.
9. Process necessity, process knockout or causal mechanism.
10. A claim that an unresolved cell is a discordance failure.
11. Exporting the Level-A envelope to cross-role, interaction or life-stage problems.
12. Claiming that convergence identifies the mechanism that caused convergence.

## Primary sentence

> Across 12 prospectively held-out taxa, independently reconstructed preserved-specimen and human-observation distribution predictions remained within prospectively frozen same-target source-discordance envelopes whenever both source-specific answers passed adequacy.

## Secondary sentence

> The result demonstrates out-of-taxon cross-source reproducibility under a frozen same-target design, not universal data-source invariance.
