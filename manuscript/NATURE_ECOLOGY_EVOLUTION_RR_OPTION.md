# Future Nature Ecology & Evolution Registered Report option

This is a separate future route from the pre-field flagship Method paper.

## Why it is relevant

Nature Ecology & Evolution accepts Registered Reports in which hypotheses, procedures, analyses and sampling plans are peer reviewed before the key data are collected. The current Level-C v8 state is unusually compatible with that logic because candidate identities, biological relation, calibration thresholds, sample minima, missingness rules and the prohibition on opening focal cross-role values before calibration are already prospectively frozen.

## Why it should not replace the current pre-field paper

The current manuscript already has a completed empirical Level-A result and a general methodological/identifiability contribution. Converting that paper into a Stage-1 Registered Report would strand the completed Level-A result and postpone publication until Stage 2.

Instead:

- Paper 1: submit the completed pre-field framework + Level-A result + Level-C identifiability boundary as a Method paper.
- Paper 2 / extension: if the Level-C field programme is made sufficiently broad and powered, consider a Nature Ecology & Evolution Registered Report Stage 1 before entering calibration data.

## Conditions before attempting the Registered Report

1. Decide whether one or both retained Level-C systems form the confirmatory Stage-1 design.
2. Add an explicit sampling/power justification for the calibration and focal endpoint, not only fixed minimum counts.
3. Freeze the Stage-2 biological endpoint and primary analysis after calibration-pass logic but before focal opening.
4. Demonstrate why the biological question is of sufficiently broad significance beyond the two systems.
5. Preserve the existing rule that failed or unavailable calibration cannot become a biological negative and does not authorize candidate replacement.

This option should remain dormant while Paper 1 is prepared; no field data should be entered merely to strengthen the pre-field manuscript.
