## Identity rule

Before upload/submission:

1. materialize the exact v0.10 manuscript from the committed builder and committed source assets;
2. record its SHA-256 hash;
3. record the release commit/tag;
4. record the archive/DOI identifier;
5. record the exact six display-file hashes;
6. confirm that the claim ledger, Supplement manifest and submission surface all point to the same release state.

Do not generate a new manuscript identity by manual editing after hashing. Any substantive edit requires rematerialization and a new identity.
