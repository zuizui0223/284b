## Placement notes

- Use **Blanchet et al. 2020** for the direct claim that co-occurrence/statistical association is not itself evidence of ecological interaction.
- Use **Galiana et al. 2024** as the complementary concession that co-occurrence can, under explicit assumptions, contain information about interaction-network structure.
- Use **Wilkinson et al. 2021** to concede that JSDMs already support joint rather than merely marginal prediction.
- Use **Gould et al. 2026** to avoid presenting prospectivity/preregistration for ecological models as novel.
